Omlette Sandwich.

Description:

A delicious and easy-to-make sandwich featuring a fluffy omelet filled with your favorite ingredients.

Ingredients:

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 slice bread
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 small onion chopped
  • 1 green chili chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Ketchup

Instructions:

  1. Beat the eggs with onion, chili, salt and pepper.
  2. Melt butter in a pan on medium heat.
  3. Pour the egg mixture into the pan and cook until set.
  4. Flip once and cook other side.
  5. Place omelette between bread slices.
  6. Add ketchup and serve.
