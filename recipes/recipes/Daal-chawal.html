Daal Chawal.

Description:

A simple and nutritious Indian dish made with lentils and rice, seasoned with aromatic spices.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup lentils
  • 2 cups rice
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • Salt to taste

Instructions:

  1. Boil lentils with turmeric and salt until soft
  2. In a separate pan fry onion in oil until golden
  3. Add cumin, chili powder and stir
  4. Pour over boiled lentils and mix
  5. Cook rice separately with salt
  6. Serve daal over rice.
