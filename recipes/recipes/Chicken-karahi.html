Chicken Karahi.

Description:

A spicy and flavorful Pakistani dish made with chicken cooked in a karahi (wok) with tomatoes, onions, and a blend of aromatic spices.

Ingredients:

  • 500g chicken
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander

Instructions:

  1. Heat oil in a wok
  2. Add chicken and fry until color changes
  3. Add ginger garlic paste and stir for 2 minutes
  4. Add tomatoes and all spices
  5. Cook on medium heat for 20 minutes until oil separates
  6. Garnish with coriander and serve with naan
